Amazon Growth Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon & Walmart Growth

Overview

Maximize Tink's peak-season performance while growing non-branded customer acquisition and strengthening the Amazon shopping experience.

Tink's already has strong brand recognition and established relevance across high-intent deer scent searches. The focus is to prepare for peak, expand search share, and make the catalog easier to discover, understand, and shop.

Tink's follows a similar seasonal strategic framework to Dead Down Wind, with both brands heavily influenced by hunting-season demand. The same overall approach of preparing ahead of peak, scaling during the strongest demand period, and reducing investment as seasonal demand declines applies, while Tink's product priorities, search opportunities, and execution remain brand-specific.

Growth Area 1: Maximize Peak-Season Demand Capture

Tink's search and sales activity builds significantly through Q3 and Q4. The priority is to enter peak with the right products, inventory, offers, and acquisition activity.

Catalog Prioritization

The #69 Doe-in-Rut range remains the hero, while newer products including Peak30 and Scrape products are showing meaningful traction.

Recommendations
  • Create Hero / Growth / Maintain / Transition ASIN classification.
  • Prioritize inventory, PPC, content, and video based on classification.
  • Protect Hero and Growth inventory throughout the peak.
  • Continue identifying emerging Growth ASINs based on search and sales performance.
Featured Offer

Some Featured Offer variability is expected due to Tink's broader off-Amazon wholesale distribution and should not be treated as a catalog-wide issue.

Recommendations
  • Monitor Featured Offer across priority ASINs, especially during peak.
  • Investigate material or unexpected changes that could limit sales or advertising.
  • Consider Featured Offer status when deciding where to scale traffic.
Brand Tailored Promotions
  • Activate ahead of hunting season and run throughout the peak.
  • Scale where performance supports continued investment.
Creator Connections
  • Evaluate Creator Connections based on profitability before activation.
  • If approved, focus activity around peak season and priority products.
  • Pause during the off-season.
Amazon Events

Prime Big Deal Days and Black Friday/Cyber Monday fall within Tink's strongest selling period.

Recommendations
  • Confirm event participation and offer strategy.
  • Prioritize Hero/Growth products based on eligibility, inventory, and profitability.
  • Align PPC and inventory planning with event activity.
  • Review event performance to inform the next seasonal cycle.
Growth Area 2: Grow High-Intent Non-Branded Search Share

Tink's already has strong branded recognition and established relevance across high-intent non-branded searches, including:

doe estrus for hunting doe urine for hunting deer scent deer lure mock scrape scent deer scrape scent

The opportunity is to reach shoppers earlier, before they have selected a brand.

Recommendations
  • Protect Tink's branded search position.
  • Prioritize high-intent non-branded searches where Tink's already demonstrates relevance.
  • Build position ahead of peak and scale as seasonal search volume increases.
  • Align SEO and PPC around the same priority search opportunities.
  • Develop additional search opportunities around Scrapes and newer products.
  • Prioritize proven high-intent searches before expanding into broader generic terms.
Advertising Strategy

Advertising follows the same seasonal logic as the rest of the Tink's plan: build search position ahead of peak, scale proven branded and non-branded opportunities through the strongest demand window, and reduce investment as search volume declines in January. Ads and SEO are aligned around the same priority searches so that paid and organic reinforce one another rather than compete.

Today, the majority of Tink's advertising results are being driven by automatic campaigns, with only a light layer of deliberate keyword targeting on top. This has captured demand efficiently, but it leaves the account under-prepared to lead into peak. The priority is to move from an automation-led setup to a deliberate structure organized around the Hero / Growth / Maintain / Transition classification, so investment concentrates behind the products and searches that matter most.

Recommendations
  • Protect branded search. Establish dedicated branded campaigns to defend high-intent Tink's searches (#69, Doe-in-Rut, #1 Doe-P, Trophy Buck, Peak30) and hold top-of-search position against competitors bidding on our name. This is efficient, high-converting demand and should not be left to automatic campaigns.
  • Build a discovery-to-performance pipeline. Use automatic and broad campaigns as a controlled discovery layer to surface new converting searches, then promote proven terms into dedicated exact-match campaigns where bids and budgets can be managed directly. Feed non-converting terms back as negatives so spend concentrates on what works.
  • Grow high-intent non-branded position ahead of peak. Prioritize the non-branded searches where Tink's already demonstrates relevance; doe estrus for hunting, doe urine for hunting, deer scent, deer lure, mock scrape scent, deer scrape scent - building rank before demand peaks rather than during it.
  • Tier investment to catalog classification. Concentrate budget behind Hero and Growth ASINs (the #69 range, Peak30, and Scrape products), with lighter maintenance spend on Maintain ASINs and minimal investment behind Transition products.
  • Develop the emerging lines deliberately. Give Scrape products and other newer items their own targeted support so they build search position as their category demand grows, rather than relying on brand-wide automatic coverage.
  • Capture competitor demand. Establish deliberate targeting against shoppers searching competing scent brands (Wildlife Research, Code Blue, Black Widow, ConQuest, Buck Bomb), where Tink's is already converting incidental traffic today.
  • Scale into events. Align advertising with Prime Big Deal Days and Black Friday/Cyber Monday, increasing investment behind Hero/Growth products during these high-demand windows.

Advertising will be managed to Tink's efficiency guardrails, with investment scaled up as seasonal demand and search volume increase, and reduced as the season winds down.

Growth Area 3: Strengthen Product Education & Guided Shopping

Tink's has a broad catalog spanning different scent types, formats, and applications. The Amazon experience should make it easier for shoppers to understand what to use, when to use it, and which product is right for them.

Main Images & Priority Listings

Main Image optimization is a priority across the catalog, beginning with Hero and Growth products.

Recommendations
  • Optimize and test Main Images across Hero/Growth ASINs to improve search-result recognition and click-through rate.
  • Optimize priority listings around the same high-intent non-branded searches identified through Search Query Performance.
  • Continue Main Image and listing optimization across the next priority ASINs.
A+ Content

Several key Tink's products are currently missing A+.

Recommendations
  • Develop A+ across Hero and Growth products.
  • Strengthen product education, usage guidance, and differentiation.
  • Cross-sell complementary products and formats.
Brand Story

Tink's currently lacks a consistent Brand Story across the catalog.

Recommendations
  • Launch an optimized Brand Story across the catalog.
  • Reinforce Tink's history and authority within the category.
  • Introduce shoppers to the broader Tink's product range.
  • Strengthen cross-product discovery.
Store

The existing Tink's Store already provides solid category navigation. The opportunity is to strengthen guided shopping rather than rebuild it.

Recommendations
  • Add stronger product-selection guidance.
  • Help shoppers navigate by scent type, hunting situation, and application.
  • Strengthen cross-selling and product-family discovery.
  • Increase visibility for newer products alongside the established #69 line.
Video
  • Develop video based on Hero/Growth priorities.
  • Focus on product use, application, product selection, and key differences between products.
DEFEND by Tink's Catalog Structure

The Coyote Urine ASINs B0GGTK9QDV and B0GGTKPP4F are currently listed under Dead Down Wind but are featured within the Tink's Store.

Recommendations
  • Confirm whether the intended brand is Tink's or Defend.
  • Determine the correct long-term Amazon structure before further investment.
  • If Dead Down Wind attribution is incorrect, evaluate relaunching under the correct brand while the listings still have limited sales and review history.
Creative Direction

Tink's has genuine heritage (since 1972, "America's #1 Buck Lure") and a differentiated trust asset most competitors lack — RT-QuIC CWD testing and RHSA Deer Protection Program certification. The catalog is also complex: multiple scent types, dispersal formats, and seasonal timing rules. The creative direction below is built around one thesis: present Tink's as a system — the right scent, in the right format, at the right time — rather than a shelf of individual products. This directly supports the Growth Area 3 goal of easier discovery and understanding, and reinforces Growth Area 2 by giving non-branded, pre-purchase shoppers a reason to trust and choose Tink's on sight.

Visual System

Carry the existing brand system onto Amazon rather than reinventing it for the channel.

Recommendations

Color-code by product line across all Amazon creative — Radiant Gold (Peak30/Natural Estrous), Pantone 186 Red (#69 Doe-in-Rut), Toyo Blue (#1 Doe-P), Charcoal Grey (Trophy Buck) — so repeat shoppers learn to navigate by color, not just text.

Use Eurostyle Black/Bold for all callouts; avoid rugged or distressed display fonts that work against the brand's modernization goal.

Surface the CWD-Tested/RHSA badge early in the shopper journey (Main Image or Image 2), not buried in A+.

Main Images (supports Hero/Growth prioritization)
Recommendations

Use product-line color as the background/accent so Hero ASINs are visually distinct in search results.

Feature one hero claim per ASIN (CWD-Tested badge or "America's #1 Buck Lure") rather than stacking multiple claims — confirm substantiation before locking any performance claim.

A+ Content
Recommendations

Open with a credibility bar: "Since 1972 / America's #1 Buck Lure / CWD-Tested & RHSA Certified."

Include a dedicated module explaining the Natural vs. Synthetic distinction — a recurring source of shopper confusion per FAQ data.

Build a scent-strategy/timing module that mirrors the secondary image sequence, reinforcing the same "system" logic.

Use a routine-builder cross-sell module (scent + dispersal method + cover scent) to directly support the "cross-sell complementary products and formats" goal already in the plan.

Brand Story
Recommendations

Lead with 1972 heritage and decades of deer-behavior expertise — Tink's clearest differentiator versus Wildlife Research, Code Blue, Black Widow, and Buck Bomb.

Follow with the modernization/innovation angle (Scrape Products, Nose Candy) to show the brand isn't resting on legacy alone.

Flag to client: no founder story or origin anecdote was provided in onboarding — a specific "how Tink's started" narrative would materially strengthen this module.

Store
Recommendations

Layer scent-type/hunting-situation/application navigation on top of the existing category structure, rather than rebuilding it — consistent with the "strengthen guided shopping, don't rebuild" direction already set.

Add a "Build Your Kit" cross-sell path (scent + dispersal + cover scent) to support AOV and routine-building.

Use the color-coded product-line system on Store navigation tiles so it reinforces the same visual logic as the listings.

Photography & Mood Direction
Recommendations

Tone: confident, credible, science-meets-tradition — trusted field expert, not tactical or rustic camo-brand energy.

Color grading: warm, natural autumn woodland tones (ties to the Radiant Gold brand color); avoid desaturated/gritty grading competitors already use.

Prioritize hands-on-product moments (applying/using scent) over posed hunter-with-bottle shots.

Avoid staged trophy-buck kill shots (Amazon content risk) and generic stock-hunter imagery.

Compliance Flags for Client Sign-Off
  • Any specific performance/effectiveness claims (e.g., "brings deer in," "#1 buck lure" used as a claim vs. product name).
  • CWD/RHSA badge usage rights and required disclaimer language.
  • Confirm no imagery or copy references glands, per existing prohibited-language guidance.
